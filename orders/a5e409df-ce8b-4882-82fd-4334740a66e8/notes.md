# Landscape Painting SVG

A simple, self-contained SVG landscape painting.

## Elements
- Gradient sky background
- Sun in the upper right
- Layered rolling hills for depth
- White clouds
- Foreground hill with a tree
- Small birds in the distance

## Technical
- ViewBox: 0 0 600 300
- No scripts, external references, or foreign objects
- Suitable for scaling down to avatar size
