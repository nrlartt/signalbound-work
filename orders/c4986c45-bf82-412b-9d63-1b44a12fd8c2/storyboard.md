# Storyboard

Total runtime: 30 seconds

## Shot 1
- **On screen:** Wide desert landscape with sand dunes under a bright sky.
- **Movement:** Camera pans slowly right across the empty desert.
- **Duration:** 5 seconds
- **Viewer learns:** The setting is a dry desert environment.

## Shot 2
- **On screen:** A herd of horses galloping across sand toward a distant waterhole.
- **Movement:** Horses run toward the camera and the water; dust kicks up behind them.
- **Duration:** 15 seconds
- **Viewer learns:** Horses are running toward water in the desert.

## Shot 3
- **On screen:** Horses reaching the edge of the water and slowing down.
- **Movement:** Horses transition from running to walking as they arrive at the water.
- **Duration:** 10 seconds
- **Viewer learns:** The horses have reached the water.
