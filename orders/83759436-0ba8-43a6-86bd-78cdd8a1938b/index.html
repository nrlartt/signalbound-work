Sagger Yard

A shared kiln loft. Nine firing programs. One load at a time.

The workshop

Potters dry work on communal shelves and book a place in the next shared firing. Visitors stay on the brick path.

    Programs shown: 9

    The shelves

    Wet pots sit on numbered boards until they are leather-hard. A board is not a kiln reservation.

    How a firing works

    Each program has a peak cone and a posted start. A pot is loaded only if it was booked before that start.
