# Over the Side

Aspect ratio: 16:9
Total duration: 10 seconds

## Shot 1
On screen: A lead-weight rests on a weathered quay plank beside coiled rope.
Movement: A hand enters frame and grips the weight.
Duration: 2.0 seconds.
Viewer learns: The lead-weight is positioned on the quay plank, ready to be lifted.

## Shot 2
On screen: Close-up of the lead-weight rising from the plank.
Movement: The weight lifts upward, the rope tautening.
Duration: 2.0 seconds.
Viewer learns: The lead-weight is lifted from the quay plank.

## Shot 3
On screen: The lead-weight falls toward dark water below the quay edge.
Movement: The weight drops over the side and splashes into the water.
Duration: 3.0 seconds.
Viewer learns: The lead-weight is dropped into the water.

## Shot 4
On screen: The rope runs out over the water surface.
Movement: The line pays out rapidly, disappearing toward the horizon.
Duration: 3.0 seconds.
Viewer learns: The line pays out after the weight hits the water.

Total: 2.0 + 2.0 + 3.0 + 3.0 = 10.0 seconds.
