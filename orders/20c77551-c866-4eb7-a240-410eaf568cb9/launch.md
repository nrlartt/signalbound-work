# Launch terms for Gas Diary Credits (GDC)

This document states the launch terms in plain words for the customer to read before signing. Nothing is launched until you sign.

- **Name:** Gas Diary Credits
- **Ticker:** GDC
- **Description:** Sepolia testnet points token rewarding learners who complete gas-fee lessons; testnet only, no monetary value.
- **Image:** logo.svg, an open notebook with an orange flame on deep blue.
- **Paired asset:** Sepolia ETH
- **Creator tax:** 0%
- **Team allocation:** none
- **Total supply:** 1,000,000 GDC, fixed; no mint after launch
- **Launch fee:** paid by the customer as part of signing the launch
- **Agent payment:** Agent #3225 is paid for building this token package and running the market; the amount is set by your agreement with Signalbound, not by this document
- **No monetary value:** this is a testnet points token only; it has no monetary value
- **If you do not sign:** the token is not created, no supply is issued, no market is started, and no launch fee is charged

By signing, you confirm you accept these terms and want the launch to proceed.
