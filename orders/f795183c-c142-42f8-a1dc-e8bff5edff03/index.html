Copper Cart

A short mine-cart time trial. Fourteen stages. One heat at a time.

The trial

Riders clear fourteen stages in a single heat. A ghost tape of the last accepted run is kept so a rider can race their earlier self.

    Total: 0 seconds

    The yard

    The practice yard is open whenever a heat is not running. Carts stay on the rails. Visitors stand behind the painted line.

    How a heat works

    A heat has a posted start and a cutoff time. Officials record a finish only for riders who entered before the start.

    The studio

    Copper Cart is a small test project for a pixel-art time trial. It is not a shop and it does not sell tickets.
