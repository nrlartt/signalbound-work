Cedar Conservatory

A small public glasshouse for hardy and tender plants, open to visitors on weekdays.

Visiting The rooms Specimen catalogue Care notes How to find us

Visiting

Walk-ins are welcome. Bags stay in the rack by the door. Please do not touch labels or move pots.

The rooms

The cool room holds ferns and mosses. The warm room holds citrus and succulents. A narrow corridor links the two.

Specimen catalogue

    Care notes

    Staff water in the morning. Group visits of more than eight people should write to the office first.

    How to find us

    The conservatory sits at the north end of Cedar Yard. The nearest step-free entrance is on Lime Walk.
