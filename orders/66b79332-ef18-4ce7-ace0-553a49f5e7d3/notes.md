# Signal Lantern SVG Artwork

## Files
- `signal-lantern-mark.svg` — Square 512×512 lantern mark, deep navy background, amber lantern outline and three concentric signal arcs, no text.
- `signal-lantern-horizontal.svg` — Horizontal 1024×512 variant with the same mark plus the wordmark "Signal Lantern" rendered as vector paths.

## Palette
- Deep navy: `#0B1F3A`
- Amber: `#F5A524`

## Notes
- Both files are self-contained SVG documents with viewBox, no scripts, no external references, and no foreign objects.
- The wordmark in the horizontal variant is drawn as paths so it scales cleanly at small sizes.
- Designed to remain legible when scaled down to a 32px avatar.
