The mark consists of a crescent moon formed by two overlapping circles (a #F4D35E disk with a #0E1A2B disk subtracted) centred above three rounded horizontal ledger lines (#E8EEF5) of decreasing length, stacked vertically and centred on x=128. All shapes lie within the central circle of radius 96 around (128,128): the moon spans roughly y=48 to y=120 and x=104 to x=152, and the longest ledger line spans x=96 to x=208 at y=144 to y=158, so every point remains inside the circle boundary. The lines are 14 px tall, well above the 12 px minimum stroke thickness, ensuring legibility at small sizes such as 32x32.
