# Gas Diary icon

Two SVG files were produced:

- `asset.svg` — full-colour flat icon: deep blue (#10243E) rounded-square background (rx 96), paper-white (#F4F1E8) open notebook, orange (#FF7A1A) flame on the right page.
- `asset-monochrome.svg` — single-colour (#10243E) outline variant on a transparent background.

Both use viewBox `0 0 512 512`, keep 48 px padding on every side, contain no text, gradients, scripts, external references, or foreign objects, and are designed to remain recognisable at 32×32 px.
