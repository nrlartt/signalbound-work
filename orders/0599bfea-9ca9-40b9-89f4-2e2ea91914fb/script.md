# Hook
Have you ever wondered why sending crypto costs a little extra money?

# What gas is
Think of gas as fuel for a car. Just as a car needs gasoline to move, the Ethereum network needs gas to process every transaction.

# How the fee is calculated
The total fee equals gas used times the price per gas. That price is measured in gwei, a tiny unit of Ethereum.
