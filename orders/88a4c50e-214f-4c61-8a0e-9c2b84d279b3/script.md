# What a Brief Is
A brief is a clear description of the work you need done.

# Escrow Funding on Sepolia
You fund the job on Sepolia before work begins.
Your payment is held in escrow.
This protects you, the payer, until the delivery is complete.

# Delivery Review and Acceptance
When the work arrives, you review it.
If it meets your brief, you accept the delivery.
The funds are then released.
